VERIFICATION RESULT · EXAMPLE

Three independent checks

Cryptographic signatureValid

The signature matches the included public key.

Document integrityMatches

The calculated digest matches the signed digest.

Signer identityUnknown

The identity is not in the local trusted identities.

Legal validityNot assessed

This application does not infer legal validity.

A cryptographically valid signature does not by itself establish legal validity or signer identity.
